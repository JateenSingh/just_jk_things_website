Passionate about bringing your ideas to life through innovative 3D printing technology. We're more than just a printing service – we're your creative partners.

Our Story

Just JK Things was born from a simple passion: the belief that everyone should have access to high-quality, custom manufacturing. What started as a hobby in a small garage has grown into a trusted 3D printing service that serves customers across South Africa.

Our founder, JK, discovered 3D printing during his engineering studies and was immediately fascinated by the technology's potential to democratize manufacturing. From creating simple prototypes to complex custom parts, we've helped thousands of customers bring their ideas to reality.

Today, we operate with state-of-the-art equipment and a commitment to quality that has earned us partnerships with major South African marketplaces including Takealot, Amazon, and Bidorbuy.
